Research Interests

  • Non-Hermitian Quantum Mechanics
  • Topological Phases of Matter
  • Floquet Systems
  • Chiral Phonons

I am a PhD Candidate at Universidad de Chile, working with Prof. Luis E. F. Foà Torres as an ANID National Doctoral Scholarship Fellow. My research engineers non-Hermitian topological phases in Floquet lattice models through protocols that alternate Hermitian and non-Hermitian dynamics — analogous to successive quantum measurements — targeting photonic-network emulators as experimental platforms.

Under the supervision of Prof. José Eduardo Barrios Vargas, in collaboration with Prof. Luis E. F. Foà Torres (Universidad de Chile).
